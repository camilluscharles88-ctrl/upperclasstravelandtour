FAQ

Questions answered.

Everything you need to know before you apply.

Can I apply regardless of my nationality?
+
Yes — absolutely. Upperclass Travel and Tour welcomes applicants from every country in the world. Whether your passport is Nigerian, Filipino, Ethiopian, Thai, Indian, Pakistani, or any other nationality, we can assist you. We have experience handling applications from over 50 nationalities.
How long does the visa process take?
+
Our document processing takes 24–48 hours from submission. Embassy processing times vary by country and visa type — tourist visas typically take 7–21 working days after your appointment. Work permits take 4–12 weeks. We provide a realistic timeline for your specific case upfront.
Do I need to visit your office in person?
+
No. Our entire service is online. You submit documents digitally, we communicate by email, and we send your completed document pack to you electronically. The only in-person requirement is your embassy appointment, which you attend locally in your home city or country.
What if my visa is rejected?
+
Our 97% success rate is the result of thorough preparation. In the rare event of rejection, we review the refusal reason with you, advise on next steps, and where appropriate, assist with a reapplication — often at a reduced service fee. We never abandon clients after a refusal.
How do you verify employers for work permit applicants?
+
We only partner with employers who are registered and licensed in their home countries. We conduct background checks and maintain direct communication with every employer before connecting any applicant. We have zero tolerance for fraudulent job offers.
Are embassy fees included in your service price?
+
No — embassy fees are separate government charges paid to the embassy or visa application centre. We pass these through at exact cost with zero markup, and we inform you of the full embassy fee for your specific visa before you proceed.
How do I communicate with my consultant?
+
All communication is conducted via email. You will be assigned a dedicated consultant who will handle your case personally and respond to all your queries by email throughout the entire process. We aim to respond to all emails within 24 hours.
Is my passport and personal information safe?
+
Absolutely. All documents are transmitted via encrypted channels and stored on secured, access-controlled servers. We comply with data protection regulations and never share your personal information with any third party without your explicit consent.
