FAQ

Questions answered.

Everything you need to know before you apply.

Can I apply regardless of my nationality?
+
Yes — absolutely. Upperclass Travel and Tour welcomes applicants from every country in the world. Whether your passport is Nigerian, Filipino, Ethiopian, Thai, Indian, Pakistani, or any other nationality, we can assist you. We have experience handling applications from over 50 nationalities.
How long does the visa process take?
+
Our document processing takes 24–48 hours from submission. Embassy processing times vary by country and visa type — tourist visas typically take 7–21 working days after your appointment. Work permits take 4–12 weeks. We provide a realistic timeline for your specific case upfront.
Do I need to visit your office in person?
+
No. Our entire service is online. You submit documents digitally, we communicate by email, and we send your completed document pack to you electronically. The only in-person requirement is your embassy appointment, which you attend locally in your home city or country.
What if my visa is rejected?
+
Our 97% success rate is the result of thorough preparation. In the rare event of rejection, we review the refusal reason with you, advise on next steps, and where appropriate, assist with a reapplication — often at a reduced service fee. We never abandon clients after a refusal.
How do you verify employers for work permit applicants?
+
We only partner with employers who are registered and licensed in their home countries. We conduct background checks and maintain direct communication with every employer before connecting any applicant. We have zero tolerance for fraudulent job offers.
Are embassy fees included in your service price?
+
No — embassy fees are separate government charges paid to the embassy or visa application centre. We pass these through at exact cost with zero markup, and we inform you of the full embassy fee for your specific visa before you proceed.
How do I communicate with my consultant?
+
All communication is conducted via email. You will be assigned a dedicated consultant who will handle your case personally and respond to all your queries by email throughout the entire process. We aim to respond to all emails within 24 hours.
Is my passport and personal information safe?
+
Absolutely. All documents are transmitted via encrypted channels and stored on secured, access-controlled servers. We comply with data protection regulations and never share your personal information with any third party without your explicit consent.

Nigeria Guide

How to Get a UK Visa from Nigeria in 2025 — Complete Guide

Getting a UK visa from Nigeria is one of the most common visa applications in West Africa — and one of the most misunderstood. With the right preparation, your chances of approval are very high. Here is everything you need to know.

Types of UK Visas Available to Nigerians

  • Standard Visitor Visa (tourist, family visit, business meetings)
  • Business Visit Visa (trade, conferences, negotiations)
  • UK Work Permit / Skilled Worker Visa (employment in the UK)
  • Student Visa (universities and colleges)

Documents Required for UK Visa from Nigeria

  • Valid Nigerian passport (must be valid for at least 6 months beyond your planned travel)
  • UK visa application form (completed online via UKVI)
  • Biometric enrolment at a VFS Global centre in Lagos, Abuja, or Port Harcourt
  • Bank statement showing at least 3–6 months of transactions from a CBN-approved Nigerian bank
  • Proof of accommodation in the UK (hotel booking or invitation letter)
  • Return flight itinerary
  • Employment letter or business registration documents
  • Recent passport photograph

How Long Does a UK Visa Take from Nigeria?

Standard processing takes 3–8 weeks after your biometric appointment. Priority processing (additional fee) can reduce this to 5–7 working days. During busy periods (June–August, December), processing can take longer.

Common Reasons for UK Visa Rejection from Nigeria

  • Insufficient funds shown in bank statement
  • Incomplete or inconsistent application form
  • Weak ties to Nigeria (no job, no property, no family)
  • Poor travel history or previous visa refusal not declared
  • Missing documents

Schengen Guide

Schengen Visa Application from Ghana — Step by Step 2025

A Schengen visa allows you to travel freely across 27 European countries with a single visa. For Ghanaians, the Schengen visa is one of the most desirable travel documents — and with the right preparation, it is very achievable.

Which Schengen Country Should Ghanaians Apply Through?

  • Apply through the embassy of the country you will spend the most time in
  • If equal time, apply through the country of first entry
  • Germany, France, and Netherlands are generally faster to process from Ghana

Documents Required for Schengen Visa from Ghana

  • Valid Ghanaian passport with at least 2 blank pages
  • Completed Schengen visa application form
  • 2 recent passport photographs (35mm x 45mm, white background)
  • Bank statement — last 3–6 months showing sufficient funds
  • Travel insurance covering €30,000 minimum for all Schengen countries
  • Hotel bookings or invitation letter
  • Return flight itinerary
  • Proof of employment (letter from employer with salary) or business registration
  • Leave approval letter from employer

Schengen Visa Processing Time from Ghana

Applications are typically processed within 15 calendar days. During peak season (June–August), processing can take up to 30 days. Apply at least 6 weeks before your travel date.

Switzerland Has the Highest Approval Rate for Ghanaians

Switzerland consistently approves over 79% of Ghanaian Schengen applications — one of the highest rates in West Africa. We recommend applying through Switzerland for first-time applicants.

Canada Guide

Canada Visa Application from Nigeria and Ghana — 2025

Canada is one of the most popular destinations for Africans — known for its high quality of life, strong African communities, and welcoming immigration policies. However, Canada visa applications require careful preparation.

Types of Canada Visas for Africans

  • Canada Tourist/Visitor Visa (most common)
  • Canada Study Permit (for universities and colleges)
  • Canada Work Permit (for skilled workers)
  • Canada Super Visa (for parents and grandparents)

Documents Required for Canada Visitor Visa from Nigeria/Ghana

  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity beyond planned stay)
  • Completed Canada visa application (online via IRCC)
  • Biometrics — must be done at a VFS Global centre
  • Bank statement showing strong financial standing
  • Proof of ties to home country (employment, property, family)
  • Invitation letter (if visiting family or friends in Canada)
  • Travel history (previous visas strengthen your application)
  • Passport photograph

How Long Does Canada Visa Take from Nigeria?

Canada visa processing typically takes 4–8 weeks from Nigeria and Ghana. During high-demand periods, it can take up to 12 weeks. Apply well in advance of your planned travel date.

Tips for a Successful Canada Visa Application

  • Show strong ties to Nigeria/Ghana — job, family, property
  • Demonstrate sufficient funds to support your stay
  • Have a clear and genuine purpose of visit
  • Previous UK or Schengen visa significantly strengthens your application
  • Apply with a professional visa agency to ensure documentation is complete

Visa Tips

Top 10 Reasons Your Visa Gets Rejected — And How to Avoid Them

Visa rejection is frustrating and costly — but most rejections are avoidable. After helping over 4,200 applicants from across Africa, here are the most common reasons we see and exactly how to avoid each one.

1. Insufficient Funds in Bank Statement

This is the number one reason for visa rejection across all countries. Embassies need to see that you can financially support yourself during your trip without working illegally.

2. Incomplete Application Form

Missing fields, inconsistent information, or errors on your application form cause immediate rejection. Every detail must match your documents exactly.

3. Weak Ties to Your Home Country

Embassies want to see that you have strong reasons to return home — a job, property, family, business. If you cannot demonstrate this, they assume you plan to overstay.

4. Previous Visa Refusal Not Declared

Many applications ask if you have been refused a visa before. Lying about this is considered fraud and results in immediate rejection and possible ban.

5. Missing or Expired Documents

  • Expired passport — must be valid for at least 6 months
  • Old photographs — must be recent (within 6 months)
  • Outdated bank statement — must show recent 3–6 months

6. No Clear Purpose of Visit

Your application must clearly explain why you are travelling, where you will stay, and how long you will be there. A vague or inconsistent explanation raises red flags.

7. Travel Insurance Not Meeting Requirements

For Schengen visas, travel insurance must cover at least €30,000 and be valid across all Schengen countries. Many applicants get rejected for insufficient coverage.

8. Applying Too Late

Applying too close to your travel date gives the embassy insufficient time and shows poor planning. Apply at least 6–8 weeks before your intended travel date.

9. Poor Travel History

Having no previous visa history makes embassies cautious. If you have been refused before, start with lower-risk countries to build your travel history.

10. Not Using a Professional Agency

Professional visa agencies know exactly what each embassy looks for and how to present your application in the strongest possible light. Our 97% success rate compared to the general average of 60–70% speaks for itself.

UK Skilled Worker Visa — How Africans Can Work in the UK Legally

Since Brexit, the UK has opened its skilled worker visa route to workers from all over the world — including Africa. This is an unprecedented opportunity for qualified and semi-qualified African workers to live and work legally in the United Kingdom.

UK Skilled Worker Visa — Who Qualifies?

  • You must have a confirmed job offer from a UK-licensed sponsor employer
  • The job must be on the UK's eligible occupations list
  • Minimum salary of £26,200 per year (or the going rate for the occupation)
  • English language requirement (B1 level or above)

Jobs in High Demand in the UK for Africans

  • Nurses and healthcare workers (NHS — very high demand)
  • Care workers and home carers
  • Construction workers and civil engineers
  • Warehouse and logistics staff
  • Teachers (especially STEM subjects)
  • IT professionals and software developers
  • Chefs and hospitality workers

Documents Required for UK Work Permit

  • Valid passport
  • Certificate of Sponsorship (provided by your UK employer)
  • Proof of English language ability
  • Proof of qualifications and work experience
  • Bank statement showing you can support yourself
  • Criminal background check from your home country
  • Tuberculosis (TB) test certificate
